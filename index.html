Adventures in Fire Island

My Trip at a Glance

Fire Island is a long, narrow barrier island off the coast of Long Island, New York, and spending a few days there felt like stepping into another world. I visited the historic Fire Island Lighthouse, walked the boardwalk through the Sunken Forest, and spent my afternoons on wide, quiet beaches backed by tall dunes.

My favorite moments were standing beside the 168-foot lighthouse, finding a rare maritime holly forest hidden behind the dunes, and watching the waves roll in with nothing around but sand and beach grass. If you love fresh sea air and peaceful scenery, this park is a must-see.
